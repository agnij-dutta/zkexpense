# Security

zkExpense is **unaudited research code**. It produces and verifies zero-knowledge proofs about payments; do not use it to protect real funds or to make compliance claims until it has been audited.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting: on https://github.com/agnij-dutta/zkexpense, open the **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. Do not open a public issue. Include the affected component, a description, and a reproduction (a failing test is ideal). You can expect an acknowledgement within a few days.

## Scope

In scope:

- **Circuits** (`circuits/lib`, the batch and aggregation wrappers): any way to produce a valid proof for a false statement (unapproved payee, payment outside the period, wrong total or budget result, a log root or hash chain that does not match the proven payments, inner proofs of a different circuit), or to learn hidden values from a proof.
- **Prover and CLI** (`src/`): mismatches between the TypeScript model and the circuit, verification that reports success for an invalid proof, leaking private inputs to disk or output.
- **Contract** (`contracts/src/ExpenseAttestation.sol`): recording a report that does not match its mandate, replaying or reordering reports, blocking an honest agent's next report, access-control bypass.

Out of scope (known non-goals, documented in the README):

- An agent omitting payments from its own log. The proof covers the log it is given; see the omission caveat and its mitigations below.
- Bugs in nargo, bb, the generated Solidity verifiers or the KZG setup themselves (please report those upstream), unless zkExpense uses them incorrectly.
- Privacy of the public inputs (payer, asset, chain, period, budget, count), which are public by design.

## Security review (2026-10-05)

An internal review covered the six areas below. It is not a substitute for an external audit.

| #   | area                        | finding                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | status                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --- | --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Sum overflow                | Amounts are `u64`, the total is a `u128` (at most 2^12 x 2^64 < 2^128) and Noir integer arithmetic is overflow checked; all integer inputs are range checked by the ABI. The sum cannot wrap the field.                                                    | Sound. Test `test_max_amounts_do_not_wrap` added.                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 2   | Padding                     | Slots past `count` are never read by any constraint and contribute leaf 0; `count <= N` is asserted.                                                                                                                                                       | Sound (`test_padding_is_ignored`, `test_zero_count_report`).                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 3   | Duplicate payments          | The same settlement tx could appear twice in a log, padding `count` and weakening the count-based omission check.                                                                                                                                          | **Fixed**: payments must be strictly increasing by `(timestamp, tx_hi, tx_lo)` with `tx_hi`/`tx_lo` as range-checked `u128`.                                                                                                  |
| 4   | Merkle domain separation    | The sponge puts the input length in its capacity lane, so 9-input leaves never collide with 2-input nodes, and every tree has fixed depth. Vendor leaves, hash-chain steps and the total commitment were 2-input hashes, the same shape as Merkle nodes.   | No exploit found (fixed depth). **Hardened**: those three now hash 3 inputs with explicit domain tags.                                                                                                                        |
| 5   | Recursive binding           | Inner public inputs are rebuilt from the outer policy and passed to `verify_proof_with_type`; the inner vk hash is pinned. Question: is the vk itself bound to the hash?                                                                                   | Tested with a real attack: an inner circuit without the vendor check, its vk, and the genuine hash. The aggregate proof does not verify (`test/adversarial/vk-substitution.test.mjs`).                                        |
| 6   | Cross-batch order           | The aggregator did not check timestamp order across batches.                                                                                                                                                                                               | **Fixed**: batch i is proven over the sub-period `[b_(i-1), b_i]` with monotonic boundaries ending at `period_end`. Residual: one payment whose timestamp equals a boundary could appear on both sides (at most K-1 repeats). |
| 7   | Period checks               | `period_start <= period_end` asserted, first payment `>= period_start`, every payment `<= period_end`, inclusive end matching the contract's `start == previous end + 1`.                                                                                  | Sound.                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 8   | Agent id squatting          | Agent ids were global and first-come: anyone could front-run `registerAgent` for a known (e.g. ERC-8004) id.                                                                                                                                               | **Fixed**: mandates are keyed by `(principal, agentId)`.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 9   | Hiding early periods        | The first report could start at any time with `chainIn = 0`, so an agent could skip a bad first month.                                                                                                                                                     | **Fixed**: `firstPeriodStart` is fixed at registration and every report must start exactly at the next expected time.                                                                                                         |
| 10  | Splitting periods           | The budget cap applied per report, so splitting a month into daily reports multiplied the effective cap.                                                                                                                                                   | **Fixed**: `minPeriodLength` per mandate.                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 11  | Non-canonical public inputs | A public input `x + p` is the same field element as `x`. bb's verifier hashes the raw bytes into the transcript, so a reused proof should fail, but a stored non-canonical `chainOut` would block the next honest report forever.                          | **Hardened**: the contract rejects any public input `>= p`.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 12  | Replay                      | Reports must start at `nextPeriodStart` and continue `lastChainOut`, so a report can be recorded at most once per mandate. The same valid proof can be recorded under another principal's mandate; that is a statement about the same agent, not a replay. | Sound (`test_submit_revertsOnReplay`, `test_submit_sameProofUnderAnotherPrincipalIsIndependent`).                                                                                                                             |
| 13  | Witness on disk             | The prover wrote the full private witness (`Prover.toml` and `.gz`) into the circuit directory and left it there.                                                                                                                                          | **Fixed**: inputs are written 0600, deleted after solving, and the witness is deleted after proving.                                                                                                                          |

## The main caveat: omitted payments

The agent writes its own log. The proof covers the payments in it and cannot see payments the agent left out, and it cannot tell a real settlement tx hash from a made-up one. What exists today:

- **Implemented**: two-way spot checks. The auditor samples on-chain transfers from the payer wallet and asks for `disclose --tx` (an omitted payment cannot be opened), and samples log positions with `disclose --index` (a fabricated entry opens to a tx that does not exist on-chain). With `k` samples, omitting a fraction `f` of payments goes unnoticed with probability about `(1 - f)^k`.
- **Implemented, weak alone**: `count` is public, so it can be compared with on-chain transfers from a dedicated payer wallet. Fabricated entries can pad it, so it is only meaningful with index spot checks.
- **Not implemented (the sound fix)**: anchor the hash chain on-chain. A settlement contract that updates the same Poseidon2 chain per payment lets `ExpenseAttestation` require `chainOut` to equal the on-chain head at period end; then omission is impossible. The circuit already exposes `chainIn`/`chainOut` and the contract already enforces continuity between reports for this.

## Trust assumptions

- Barretenberg's UltraHonk prover, recursive verifier and generated Solidity verifiers are correct, and the Aztec Ignition KZG setup was honest.
- The `ExpenseAttestation` owner can add verifiers (never replace one) and pause submissions. Put it behind a multisig or timelock.
- The principal chooses honest mandate parameters; a cap of `type(uint96).max` or a `minPeriodLength` of 0 disables those checks.
